# Cahit Moderasyon — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 13, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Cahit Moderasyon is a Discord moderation bot designed to help server administrators manage their communities, enforce server rules, and maintain a safe environment.

## 2. Information We Process
Depending on enabled features, the bot may process:

- Discord user, server, channel, and role IDs
- Member join and leave events
- Moderation actions, including bans, kicks, warnings, and timeouts
- Message content when required for moderation or command processing
- Server settings and permission information
- Moderation logs and relevant timestamps

## 3. How Information Is Used
Information is processed to execute moderation commands, enforce configured server rules, detect prohibited content, maintain moderation records, and support server administration.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention
Moderation records and server settings may be stored to support bot functionality. Data retention periods depend on the bot's actual configuration and storage practices.

## 5. Data Sharing
Cahit Moderasyon does not sell user data. Moderation information may be accessible to authorized server administrators and necessary service providers.

## 6. Data Deletion
Users may request information about stored personal data or request deletion through the bot's official support contact, subject to applicable requirements.

## 7. Security
Reasonable security measures are used to protect information processed by the bot.

## 8. Policy Updates
This Privacy Policy may be updated when features or data practices change.

## 9. Contact
For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Cahit Moderasyon developer through the official support contact.
